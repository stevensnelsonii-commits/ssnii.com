Services

What the studio does

Client-shaped work, in three trades. If it needs doing right and it needs one accountable pair of hands, it belongs here.

01

Environment Art & Art Direction

Twenty-five years of building worlds that hold up at gameplay distance and in the screenshot alike. The specialty is materials and texture — the way a surface tells you its history — scaled from a single set piece to entire cities.

  • World building — cities, interiors, and landscapes, blocked out to final
  • Modular kits — the systems whole zones get built from
  • Art direction — look development, style guides, and team direction
  • Track record — Vivec City and the Imperial City in Elder Scrolls Online; every structure in Summerset; Warhammer Online city architecture

02

Illustration

Creatures, characters, and dark little worlds, drawn by hand, all digital. Built for print — covers and interiors that still read at thumbnail size and reward a second look up close.

  • Book covers & interiors — including illustration work for the Extinction Cycle
  • Creature & character design — concept to finished piece
  • Editorial & spot art — one strong image that carries the page
  • Process, on camera — process videos available for releases that want the making-of

03

Software

The studio designs and builds the tools it wishes existed — then ships them to clients with the same problem. Software shaped around the people who actually use it, not around the demo.

  • Cadence — a scheduling platform built for Warren County Summer Music School, built to kill the yearly data-entry grind
  • SwampShell Prime — the studio's own production system: projects, tasks, and follow-through in one place
  • Custom tools & sites — small, sharp software for small teams (this site included)
