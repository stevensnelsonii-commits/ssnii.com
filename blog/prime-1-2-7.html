Company News — SwampShell Prime

Building Prime in Public: 1.2.7

The painted bird lands, fullscreen works the way it should, and a beta is on the horizon.

November 7, 2026 · Bel Air, Maryland

Company news, straight from the build machine: SwampShell Prime 1.2.7 is built, installed, and verified on the studio's machine today. Two changes in this build — and the first one matters more than it sounds.

The bird is the real bird now. Since the earliest builds, Prime has worn a simplified, traced stand-in for the Swamp Shell mark. As of 1.2.7 the app carries the real thing: the full painted shell-bird — the cracked spiral shell, the goggle eye, the beak — in the header, on the welcome screen, and as the app's favicon. It's a painting, made by hand, and it's the same mark that's on the studio wall. A studio runs on its mark; the software should wear the real one.

Fullscreen works like it should. Prime is a working tool, and working tools need to get out of the way. The window now maximizes and goes truly fullscreen the way a desktop app is supposed to — the whole screen for the board, nothing between you and the day's work.

For anyone just arriving: Prime is the app we're building to run this one-person studio. A morning board that deals the day's quests in order. Tasks, jobs, and money in one place, because a studio this size can't afford to keep its books in its head. A character creator, a 3D playground, and an AI inside the app — also named Prime — whose job is to help run the board. It's built here, used here every day, and the process gets published as it goes. We publish process, never position.

Where this is headed: a public beta. The next milestone isn't a feature. It's people. Prime is opening to a first beta cohort, and the goal for that cohort is 1,000 users. There is no download link yet — and there won't be one until the build is ready to be judged. Beta info is coming soon. When it lands, the people on the list hear first.
